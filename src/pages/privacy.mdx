# Demo data handling notice

**Template notice / Demonstration only**

Kojima is an interface template for a corporate intelligence product concept. Its scenarios are synthetic. This site does not operate an intelligence service, create accounts, or provide a secure workspace for confidential information.

This notice describes the supplied demo behavior. It is not a production privacy policy.

## Theme preference

The theme switch stores your light or dark preference in your browser's local storage under the key `theme`. The site reads that preference on later visits. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser storage. Theme switching also works when local storage is unavailable, but the preference may not persist.

## Demonstration forms

The contact, sign-in, and registration forms process their demo interactions locally in your browser. Their submission handlers do not send entered values over the network or connect to an account or messaging service.

Sign-in and registration validate the form and clear it after successful validation; they do not authenticate you or save credentials. The contact form prepares an inquiry in page memory. If you choose to download it, a text file is saved to your device. That file remains until you remove it.

Use sample information and a made-up password. Browser autofill, password managers, and downloaded files are controlled by your browser or device separately from this template.

## Hosting and page requests

Loading a hosted copy of this site makes network requests for pages and assets. The hosting provider may record request information such as IP addresses, requested paths, timestamps, and browser details. Logging, retention, and any additional analytics depend on the deployment; this template does not establish those settings.

Following an external link takes you to another site with its own data handling practices.

## Before using this as a real product

A site operator must replace this template notice with information that reflects the deployed service, its integrations, hosting configuration, and actual data handling. Adding forms that send data, authentication, analytics, or other services changes the behavior described here.

For the current demo's scope, see the [frequently asked questions](/faq).
